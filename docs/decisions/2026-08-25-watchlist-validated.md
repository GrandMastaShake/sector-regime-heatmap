## Finding 1: SPCX has insufficient history for the month horizon

SPCX is real. SpaceX completed its IPO on 2026-06-12 and trades on Nasdaq.
As of the 2026-08-24 research snapshot that is roughly 50 sessions.

Against `docs/metric_definitions.md` this means SPCX cannot supply
`three_month_relative_return_pct`, and its `relative_volume_ratio` is
unreliable while the rolling baseline still contains IPO-week volume from a
75B USD offering. `trend_participation_pct` depends on the logged threshold; a
50-day one is only just satisfied, a 200-day one is not.

SPCX is the largest constituent of the Industrials basket. So one of ten
equal-weight names cannot supply month-horizon inputs. Industrials must either
exclude SPCX at that horizon and record a denominator of 9, or set
`data_quality.status` to `warn`. It must not compute over 9 while reporting as
if over 10 - that is the same silent-denominator failure as a dropped
component.

Recorded in `config/watchlist_overrides.yaml`. Preflight prints the constraint
and the date it clears (2026-09-11) for every snapshot it still applies to.

## Finding 2: the CapTier column is not an ordering

26 of 110 names carry a tier label smaller than a name with a lower market cap.
LIN at 225.91B is Mega while AMD at 771.68B is Large. COST at 420.30B is Large
while CAT at 381.36B is Mega. CEG at 97.32B is Mid while LNG at 58.15B is Large.

Nothing in the scoring path reads CapTier today, so this is latent rather than
live. But `docs/methodology.md` lists one-name concentration in the risk
penalty, and cap tier is the obvious thing to reach for when that gets built.

The column stays in the CSV as transcribed - the CSV is a faithful copy of the
spreadsheet and is never hand-edited. Tier is instead derived from explicit
boundaries in `config/watchlist_overrides.yaml` (Mega 300B, Large 100B, Mid
20B, Micro 0), which relabels 13 names. Preflight enforces that the boundaries
decrease and that the derived tiers do not overlap, and prints the divergence
count so it stays visible.

The boundaries are a judgment call. A count-preserving alternative that keeps
the original 18/38/34/20 split needs cut points at 408.76 / 86.22 / 22.42 and
relabels only 10 names, but the boundaries are arbitrary artifacts of this
particular snapshot. Round numbers were chosen because they survive the next
market move. Change them in the overrides file if you disagree; the gate holds
either way.
